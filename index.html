Beginner's Guide to Badminton



Basic Rules
  • Each game is played to 21 points
  • The serve must be travelled diagonally
  • The serve must hit underhand, below the waist
  • You cannot touch the net while the shuttlecock is in play.
Badminton Equipment
  • Racket
  • Shuttlecock
  • Shoes
Basic Techniques
  • Forehand grip
  • Backhand grip
  • Ready stance
  • Low and high serve
